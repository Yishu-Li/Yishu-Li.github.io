## 臭嘴(chòu zuǐ)

## Stinky Mouth

#### Principle Investigator, She's the boss

Stinky Mouth adopted me as her poop-picker in 2024 when I was working in Shanghai. She was wandering around my apartment and one day she came home with me.

Stinky Mouth is the greatest cat of all time (GOAT). She never scratches anyone, and she's a pro at using her litter box. She's super friendly to everyone, even strangers. She loves to sleep with me, stay up late to watch Premier League games, and share my Lay's potato chips!
