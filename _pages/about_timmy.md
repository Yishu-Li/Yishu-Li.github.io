## 小胆(xiǎo dǎn)

## Timmy (from the word Timid)

Timmy is the daughter of Stinky Mouth. Although Stinky Mouth is a brave cat who once dared to fight a dog larger than herself while wandering around my house before living with me, Timmy is so timid that she runs away from anything or anyone except her mom.

Timmy likes to play with balls and cables, and she's always hiding from everyone. However, she's so pretty and has silky black fur.
