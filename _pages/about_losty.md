## 丢丢(diū diū)

## Losty (Because we thought she was abandon by her mom)

My parents found Losty at a mall near our home in Xiangyang, Hubei, and chose to adopt her. Although she shares the same fur color and closely resembles Stinky Mouth, they are not mother and daughter.

Losty is always energetic and curious about everything. She sometimes enjoys annoying others, especially Stinky Mouth, which is probably why Stinky Mouth doesn't really like her.
